Frequently Asked

How do I list my club's event on the hub?
Tech club coordinators can request organizer access through the Contact form — our team verifies and onboards clubs within 2 working days.
I can't find my department in the filters.
Write to us with your department name and we'll add it to the platform within the week.
Is this platform limited to one college?
Currently yes — Smart Technical Events Hub is built for single-campus deployment as a final-year academic project.
