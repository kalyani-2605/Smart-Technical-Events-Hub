CodeStorm Hackathon 2026

A 36-hour campus hackathon where teams of four design and ship working prototypes for real problems faced on campus — from smart attendance to accessible navigation.

Register Now

About the Event

CodeStorm is the CSE department's flagship hackathon, open to all engineering branches. Teams of up to four will have 36 hours to design, build and pitch a working prototype addressing one of three campus-life tracks: Smart Campus, Accessibility, and Sustainability. Mentors from partner companies will be available throughout, and the top three teams receive cash prizes plus fast-tracked interview slots with sponsor companies.

Eligibility

  • Open to all currently enrolled undergraduate students.
  • Teams of 2–4 members; solo entries not permitted.
  • At least one team member must be from a core engineering branch.
  • Laptop and required hardware to be arranged by participants.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can first-year students participate?
Yes — CodeStorm is open to students from all years, as long as team eligibility rules are met.
Is there a registration fee?
No, participation is completely free for all enrolled students.
Will food and accommodation be provided?
Meals and refreshments are provided throughout the 36 hours. The venue stays open overnight for continuous hacking.
Do I need a finished idea before applying?
No — problem statements are released at the opening ceremony. Come with your team and your skills.
