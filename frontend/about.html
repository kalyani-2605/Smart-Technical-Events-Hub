Why we built this

One Hub for Every Technical Opportunity on Campus

Smart Technical Events Hub started as a final-year project to solve a problem every engineering student knows: technical events are everywhere — WhatsApp groups, noticeboards, department circulars — but never in one place.

Our Mission

To make sure no engineering student misses a hackathon, workshop or internship drive simply because they didn't hear about it in time.

Our Vision

A campus where discovering, registering for, and tracking technical events is as effortless as checking a single dashboard.

Our Objectives

Centralize event listings, simplify registration for organizers, and give students a portable record of everything they've participated in.

Built to Scale

Designed to support every department's tech club, from a 40-person workshop to a 1500-student annual fest.

Why students use it

Benefits

Every event, one place

No more scattered posters and forwarded messages — every technical event across every department, searchable in one hub.

One-click registration

Your profile details are saved once — registering for a new event takes seconds, not a fresh Google Form each time.

A permanent certificate wallet

Every certificate you earn stays attached to your profile — downloadable and shareable whenever you need it.

Easier for organizers too

Tech clubs get a ready-made registration page and live seat tracking without building anything from scratch.
